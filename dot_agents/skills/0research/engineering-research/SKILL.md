# Engineering Research

Pre-process: Ensure I have did some `memory_search` on my local trusted knowledge base.

## Process

### Phase 1: Initial Broad Research

Conduct **3 searches** with:
- Different keywords in English
- Varying search depths (recent vs. comprehensive)
- Mix of exact phrases and broader terms

Sources: Search engines, Documentations, Reddit, GitHub Issues, Stack Overflow

### Phase 2: Deep Dive

Based on Phase 1 findings, conduct **5 additional searches** focusing on:
- Specific error messages or edge cases
- Real-world usage patterns
- Community discussions and workarounds

### Phase 3: Multi-language Research (if needed)

For Asian tech contexts or localized content:
- **3 preliminary searches** in Chinese (Baidu, Chinese forums)
- **5 in-depth searches** for detailed solutions

## Principles

- Engineering thinking
- Delve into multiple sources (Reddit, GitHub Issues, forums) for the fullest picture
- Look for community-validated solutions over documentation alone
- Consider version-specific issues and compatibility
